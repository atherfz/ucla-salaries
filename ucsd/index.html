About this data: Pay figures come directly from UC's own public payroll disclosure. Names shown as ***** are redacted by UC itself under federal student-privacy law (applies to anyone whose employment depended on student status at any point that year, including medical residents/fellows).

Department / Specialty is not part of UC's official data. It was inferred by cross-referencing each name against the public NPI Registry (a federal database of licensed healthcare providers), and for a smaller number of names with no confident registry match, by an exact first+last name match against CMS's public National Downloadable File (Medicare enrollment records). Entries marked with † had more than one plausible name match in the registry and carry more name-collision risk than the rest. Rows with no department/specialty at all mean no confident match was found by either method — roughly 1,300 of the ~4,600 unique named people in this dataset — often because the person is non-clinical/research faculty who don't see patients, doesn't bill Medicare under their own NPI, or has since left UCSD and no longer appears in current directories. This is a best-effort estimate, not an official UCSD record, and should not be treated as verified identification of any individual's medical specialty.
